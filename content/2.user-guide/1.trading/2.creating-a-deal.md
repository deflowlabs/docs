Creating a deal records proposed terms and sends them to a counterparty. It does not deploy or fund an escrow by itself.

## Readiness checklist

- Both participants are using dedicated Sepolia test wallets.
- Your account shows the required readiness checks as complete.
- The settlement token is the allowlisted TestUSDC address shown on [Fees and supported assets](/user-guide/trading/fees-and-supported-assets).
- The counterparty identifier was obtained through a trusted channel.

## Enter and review the terms

1. Open **Deals** and choose **Create deal**.
2. Select the buyer and seller roles carefully.
3. Enter the TestUSDC amount and any displayed commercial terms.
4. Review the funding deadline, dispute window, and allocation of fees.
5. Confirm the counterparty and send the invitation.

::callout{type="tip"}
Share the deal link through a separate trusted channel and ask the counterparty to compare the key terms before accepting it.
::

## Before accepting

The counterparty should verify the roles, test-asset contract, amount, timing, and fee information. If anything is wrong, reject or cancel the proposal and create a corrected one. Do not rely on an informal message to modify accepted terms.
