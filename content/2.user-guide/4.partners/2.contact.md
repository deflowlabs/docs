For partnership enquiries, technical support, or general questions, use the channels listed below.

## Partnership Enquiries

If you are interested in the DeFlow Partner Program, White-Label opportunities, or institutional onboarding:

- **Email**: [partners@deflowlabs.io](mailto:partners@deflowlabs.io)
- **Contact form**: [deflowlabs.io/contact](https://deflowlabs.io/contact) (select "Partnership" as the enquiry type)

Please include your company name, intended use case, and expected monthly volume in your message. The partnerships team typically responds within two business days.

## Technical Support

For platform issues, account questions, or deal-related support:

- **Email**: [support@deflowlabs.io](mailto:support@deflowlabs.io)
- **Contact form**: [deflowlabs.io/contact](https://deflowlabs.io/contact) (select "Support" as the enquiry type)

When reporting an issue, include:

- Your username (not your wallet address or private key)
- A description of what you expected to happen and what occurred
- The deal ID, if applicable
- Screenshots, if relevant

::callout{type="warning"}
DeFlow team members will never ask for your private key, recovery phrase, or wallet password. Do not share sensitive wallet information with anyone, regardless of who they claim to be.
::
