## Support

Email [support@deflowlabs.io](mailto:support@deflowlabs.io) for account, readiness, or workflow help. Include the approximate time, visible status, deal ID, and public transaction hash when relevant.

## Partnerships

Email [partners@deflowlabs.io](mailto:partners@deflowlabs.io) for partner onboarding or institutional access enquiries.

## Security

Email [security@deflowlabs.io](mailto:security@deflowlabs.io) privately for suspected vulnerabilities.

::callout{type="danger"}
DeFlow will not ask for a seed phrase, private key, wallet recovery code, or identity document by email. Redact unrelated personal information from screenshots and logs.
::
