The partner programme is intended for introducers, brokers, desks, and ecosystem organisations that refer eligible participants. Access, attribution, rebates, and benefits are subject to onboarding and current product readiness.

## Current scope

- Partner candidates can discuss onboarding and attribution with DeFlow.
- Eligible referred activity can use the configured referral and rebate logic in the current release.
- Dashboards, custom schedules, co-marketing, white-labelling, and other tier benefits may be limited or planned even when they appear in internal commercial models.

## Commercial schedule

The internal partner model uses lifetime referred volume and six rebate tiers. A proposed tier or projection is not a binding commercial offer. Final terms require written approval and must identify which benefits are operational.

## Responsible partner communication

Partners must use the current availability labels and deployment facts when describing DeFlow. They must not promise returns, mainnet settlement, certified compliance, an independent audit, or availability of roadmap features.

For onboarding, use the [partner contact details](/user-guide/partners/contact).
