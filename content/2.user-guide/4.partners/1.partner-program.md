The DeFlow Partner Program is designed for brokers, introducers, institutional allocators, and ecosystem builders who drive settlement volume through the platform. Partners earn progressive fee rebates based on the lifetime volume of traders they introduce.

## Partner Types

| Type | Description | Typical Profile |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Broker / Introducer** | Introduces traders to DeFlow and earns rebates on their settlement fees | Independent brokers, OTC desks, trading communities |
| **Institutional Allocator** | Brings institutional flow from funds, family offices, and syndicates | Fund administrators, capital allocators, syndicate leads |
| **Ecosystem Builder** | Integrates DeFlow into their platform or product | DeFi protocols, portfolio trackers, wallet providers |
| **White-Label Operator** | Deploys a fully branded DeFlow instance for their own clients | Banks, prime brokers, regulated exchanges |

## Onboarding Process

::step-guide

:::step{title="Application" n="1"}
Submit an application through the partner portal with your company details, intended use case, and expected volume.
:::

:::step{title="Review and KYB Verification" n="2"}
The DeFlow compliance team reviews your application. Know Your Business (KYB) verification is conducted through DeFlow's identity verification engine, including entity verification, director checks, and AML screening.
:::

:::step{title="Desk Assignment" n="3"}
Upon approval, you receive a dedicated Smart Desk with configurable branding (logo, colours, vanity URL) and API keys where applicable.
:::

:::step{title="Attribution Setup" n="4"}
A unique attribution code is assigned to your account. All deals from traders you introduce are automatically linked to your partner profile, and volume tracking begins immediately.
:::

:::step{title="Go Live" n="5"}
Start introducing traders. Rebates accumulate automatically on every settled deal. Your partner dashboard provides real-time KPI tracking.
:::

::

## Rebate Schedule

Partners earn progressive fee rebates based on **lifetime referred volume**, which is the total notional value of deals settled by traders they introduced.

| Partner Tier | Lifetime Referred Volume | Fee Rebate | Effective Rate (on 0.55% base) |
|:-------------|:-------------------------|:----------:|:------------------------------:|
| **Entry** | Under $1M | 0% | 0.55% |
| **Active** | $1M to $10M | 5% | 0.5225% |
| **Established** | $10M to $50M | 10% | 0.495% |
| **Senior** | $50M to $250M | 15% | 0.4675% |
| **Enterprise** | $250M to $1B | 20% | 0.44% |
| **Strategic** | Over $1B | 25% | 0.4125% |

### Rebate Rules

- **Mutual exclusivity**: Partner rebates and VIP discounts do not stack. Only the higher of the two is applied to any given deal
- **Revenue floor**: The effective fee after any discount cannot drop below **0.10%**
- **Lifetime accumulation**: Referred volume never resets. Once a partner reaches a tier, it is retained permanently
- **Permanent attribution**: Once a trader is attributed to a partner, all future deals by that trader count toward the partner's volume

## Dashboard and KPIs

Partners have access to a dedicated dashboard with the following metrics:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Lifetime Referred Volume** | Total notional value of deals settled by referred traders |
| **Current Tier** | Current rebate tier and progress toward the next |
| **Active Traders** | Number of referred traders who have settled at least one deal |
| **Monthly Volume** | Current month's referred settlement volume |
| **Rebate Savings** | Total fee savings from partner rebate (lifetime) |
| **Conversion Rate** | Funnel from Invited to Registered to First Trade to Active |

## White-Label Upgrade Path

Partners who reach the **Strategic tier** ($1B+ lifetime referred volume) become eligible for the White-Label Program, which provides a fully branded DeFlow instance under the partner's own domain.

| Feature | Partner Program | White-Label |
|---------|:--------------:|:-----------:|
| Branding | Desk branding within DeFlow | Fully standalone branded instance |
| Domain | deflowlabs.io/desk/firm-name | firm.platform.com |
| User management | Shared DeFlow user pool | Isolated user base |
| Fee schedule | Partner rebate tiers | Custom fee schedule (negotiated) |
| Infrastructure | Shared platform infrastructure | Dedicated or shared (by arrangement) |
