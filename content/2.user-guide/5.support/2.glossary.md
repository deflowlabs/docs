A reference of key terms and concepts used throughout this documentation. Terms are listed alphabetically.

## A

### AML (Anti-Money Laundering)

A set of laws, regulations, and procedures designed to prevent criminals from disguising illegally obtained funds as legitimate income. On DeFlow, AML screening is conducted by a third-party provider and must be current (within 24 hours) before a user can fund a deal.

### Atomic Settlement

A settlement mechanism where all components of a transaction execute as a single, indivisible operation. Either all transfers complete, or none do. DeFlow implements atomic settlement through smart contract escrow, ensuring that both sides of a deal receive their assets in the same transaction.

### Attribution

The permanent link between a referred trader and the partner or referrer who introduced them to the platform. Once attribution is established, it cannot be changed, and all future deals by the referred trader contribute to the referrer's or partner's lifetime volume.

## B

### Blueprints

Saved deal templates that allow users at Gold II tier and above to create new deals based on previously used configurations. Blueprints reduce the time required to set up recurring deal types.

## C

### Concurrent Deal Limit

The maximum number of deals a user can have open simultaneously. This limit is determined by VIP tier, ranging from 3 (Bronze) to unlimited (Obsidian and above).

### CREATE2

An Ethereum opcode that enables deterministic smart contract address computation. DeFlow uses CREATE2 in the EscrowFactory to generate escrow addresses that can be verified by both parties before deployment.

## D

### DvP (Delivery-versus-Payment)

A settlement procedure where the transfer of one asset occurs simultaneously with the transfer of the other. DeFlow enforces DvP at the smart contract level, eliminating the risk of one party defaulting after receiving the counterparty's assets.

### Desk

A configurable workspace on the platform that can represent a trading team, brokerage, or partner organisation. Desks can have custom branding, member management, and dedicated deal pipelines.

### Dispute Window

An optional, configurable period after both sides of a deal have funded during which either party can raise a dispute. If a dispute is raised, settlement pauses until it is resolved.

## E

### EIP-1167

An Ethereum Improvement Proposal that defines the minimal proxy contract pattern. DeFlow uses EIP-1167 to deploy gas-efficient escrow instances that delegate all logic to a shared implementation contract.

### ERC-20

A technical standard for fungible tokens on the Ethereum blockchain. Most tokens traded on DeFlow (USDC, WBTC, etc.) conform to this standard.

### Escrow

A financial arrangement where a third party (in this case, a smart contract) holds assets on behalf of two transacting parties until the terms of the agreement are fulfilled. On DeFlow, each deal has its own dedicated escrow contract.

### EscrowFactory

The smart contract responsible for creating new escrow instances. It uses EIP-1167 minimal proxies and CREATE2 for gas-efficient, deterministic deployment.

## F

### Fee Credits

A reward balance earned through the referral programme. Credits are applied automatically as a discount of up to 50% on the platform fee when settling a deal. Credits expire 12 months after the referral link was established.

### FeeRouter

The smart contract that calculates and distributes platform fees at settlement. It applies tier discounts, referral credits, and partner rebates, then routes the net fee to the platform treasury and any applicable broker wallets.

### Forge API

DeFlow's programmatic interface available to users at Gold III tier and above. The Read tier allows data export; the Execute tier allows programmatic deal management.

## G

### Governance Multisig

A Safe{Wallet} multisig wallet that controls administrative functions on the DeFlow smart contracts, including emergency pause, dispute resolution, and factory upgrades. No single key holder can execute administrative actions unilaterally.

## H

### HMAC-SHA256

A cryptographic hashing algorithm used by DeFlow to irreversibly hash email addresses before storage. The original email cannot be recovered from the hash.

## K

### KYB (Know Your Business)

An identity verification process for corporate entities. Required for partner programme onboarding, including entity verification, director checks, and AML screening.

### KYC (Know Your Customer)

An identity verification process for individual users. Required before creating or participating in deals on DeFlow. Conducted by Zyphe, a certified third-party provider.

### Kickback

The percentage of a platform fee that is credited to a referrer when their referred user settles a deal. The kickback rate is 5% (Standard) or 10% (Master Rev-Share).

## L

### Leaderboard

A public ranking of platform users by lifetime settled volume and deal count. Rankings display only usernames and VIP tier badges. Users can opt out from Settings.

## M

### Master Rev-Share

An elevated referral commission rate (10% instead of 5%) unlocked at Platinum III (Rank 12). Applies to all future referral earnings.

## N

### Non-Custodial

A system design where the platform never holds or controls user funds. On DeFlow, all assets are held in smart contract escrows controlled by the deal terms, not by the platform.

## O

### OTC (Over-the-Counter)

A method of trading financial instruments directly between two parties, outside of a formal exchange. DeFlow provides the settlement infrastructure for OTC trades.

## P

### Payout Isolation

A design pattern where the failure of a secondary payout (such as a broker commission) cannot cause the core settlement transaction to revert. Core deal execution always completes.

### Platform Fee

The fee charged by DeFlow on each settled deal, typically 0.50% of the notional value. Reduced by VIP tier discounts, referral credits, and partner rebates.

### Privy

The authentication provider used by DeFlow for secure, passwordless wallet connection and embedded wallet management.

### Pulse Analytics

Advanced trading metrics and market insights available to users at Gold I tier and above.

## R

### Referral Code

A unique, alphanumeric code assigned to every DeFlow user (format: NX followed by 8 characters). Used to invite new traders and earn referral rewards.

### Revenue Floor

The minimum platform fee percentage (0.10%) that applies regardless of any discounts, credits, or rebates.

## S

### Settlement

The final stage of a deal lifecycle where the escrow contract executes the atomic swap, delivering assets to both parties and routing fees to the platform.

### Shadow Identity

A mechanism that preserves referral attribution for users who receive a referral link but have not yet created a DeFlow account. When they register, the referral link activates retroactively.

### Smart Wallet

An ERC-4337 account abstraction wallet provisioned for each DeFlow user, enabling gasless transactions, batched operations, and session keys.

### Syndicate

A pooled investment structure where multiple participants contribute capital toward a single deal. The syndicate lead sets the terms and manages allocations.

## V

### VIP Tier

A rank in the DeFlow progression system determined by lifetime settled volume. Tiers unlock fee discounts, higher deal limits, and exclusive platform features.

## W

### White-Label

A fully branded DeFlow instance deployed under a partner's own domain, available to Strategic tier partners ($1B+ lifetime referred volume).

## Z

### Zero-PII

DeFlow's data protection policy that prevents any personally identifiable information from being stored in the platform database. All personal data is either hashed irreversibly or handled exclusively by certified third-party providers.

### Zyphe

The certified identity verification provider used by DeFlow for KYC and KYB checks. Zyphe handles all personal documents and biometric data; DeFlow receives only verification status flags.
