## Reading the Table

Each column represents a specific parameter:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Rank** | Sequential position (1 through 19) |
| **Tier** | Named tier with sub-level (I, II, or III) |
| **Volume Threshold** | Minimum lifetime settled volume required to reach this rank |
| **Concurrent Deals** | Maximum number of deals you can have open simultaneously |
| **Fee Discount** | Percentage reduction applied to the base platform fee |
| **Commission Rate** | Referral kickback rate (percentage of platform fees from referred deals) |
| **Credit/Cash Split** | How referral earnings are divided between fee credits and cash balance |
| **Monthly Cap** | Maximum referral earnings permitted per calendar month |
| **Key Perk** | The primary new feature or benefit unlocked at this rank |

## Important Rules

- **Lifetime accumulation**: Volume is cumulative and never resets. Every settled deal adds to your lifetime total.
- **No demotion**: Once you reach a rank, you retain it permanently. Your tier cannot decrease.
- **Automatic advancement**: The platform recalculates your tier after every settled deal. No manual action is required.
- **Leaderboard display**: Your tier badge appears next to your username on the leaderboard and in deal activity.
