DeFlow calculates VIP rank from lifetime settled volume. Each rank defines a threshold, fee discount, concurrent-deal limit, referral commission, reward split, monthly cap, and an intended product benefit. The values below come from the canonical product ledger and runtime constants.

::vip-ranks
::

## How VIP ranks work

- Rank progression is calculated after eligible settled deals.
- A fee-discount percentage is a reduction from the applicable base rate, not the final fee rate.
- Referral commission, credit/cash split, monthly caps, and concurrent-deal limits are enforced economic controls in the current implementation.
- Named benefits are the approved entitlement design. Availability remains feature-specific until the VIP Perk Engine verifies and gates each benefit at runtime.

::callout{type="warning"}
The current release uses Sepolia test volume and TestUSDC. These records have no monetary or investment value, and migration into a later release is not guaranteed.
::
