The DeFlow interface is organised around a small set of stable product areas. Availability labels identify areas whose current behaviour remains limited.

## Dashboard

The dashboard summarises account readiness, active deals, recent events, lifetime volume, and rewards. In the current release, volume and rewards are test records rather than financial statements.

## Deals

Use the Deals area to choose an OTC trade or Syndicate raise. OTC status labels should match the [OTC deal lifecycle](/user-guide/trading/otc-deal-lifecycle). The Syndicate wizard captures caps, minimum ticket, deadline, access mode and FIFO allocation rules; see the [Syndicate overview](/user-guide/syndicates/overview).

## Rewards

The rewards area shows your referral code, fee-credit balance, TestUSDC reward balance, VIP rank, and withdrawal eligibility. See [VIP tiers](/user-guide/rewards/vip-tiers) and [Referral programme](/user-guide/rewards/referral-program).

## Settings and readiness

Settings contains your public username, wallet connection, leaderboard preference, and available account controls. Readiness indicators show whether identity, policy, and wallet checks allow the next protected action.

## Help

If an on-screen status differs from this documentation, preserve the transaction hash or visible error and use [Troubleshooting](/user-guide/support/troubleshooting) before retrying.
