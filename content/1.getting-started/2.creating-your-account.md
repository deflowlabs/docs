Setting up your DeFlow account takes less than two minutes. The platform uses wallet-based, passwordless authentication, meaning you do not need a traditional username and password. Your wallet is your identity.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have the following:

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- A cryptocurrency wallet (any browser extension wallet, WalletConnect-compatible wallet, or mobile wallet)
- An invite code from the DeFlow team or an existing user (required during Private Beta)

## Account Creation Process

::step-guide

:::step{title="Visit the Platform" n="1"}
Navigate to the DeFlow application at the URL provided in your invite communication. The platform is accessible from any modern desktop or mobile browser.
:::

:::step{title="Connect Your Wallet" n="2"}
Click the **Connect Wallet** button in the top-right corner of the screen. DeFlow supports secure, passwordless authentication. You can connect using:

- **Browser extension wallets** such as MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, or any compatible extension
- **WalletConnect** for mobile wallets
- **Email or social login** which creates an embedded wallet managed securely on your behalf

Select your preferred method and follow the prompts to authorise the connection.
:::

:::step{title="Enter Your Invite Code" n="3"}
During the Private Beta, access requires a valid invite code. Enter the code you received when prompted. If you do not have an invite code, you can request one from an existing user or through the [waitlist](https://deflowlabs.io/waitlist).
:::

:::step{title="Set Your Username" n="4"}
Choose a display name that will represent you across the platform, including the leaderboard and deal activity. This is the only identifying information stored by DeFlow. No real name, email, or personal data is required or retained. See the [Zero-PII Policy](/user-guide/security/zero-pii-policy) for details.
:::

:::step{title="Complete Identity Verification" n="5"}
Before you can create or participate in deals, you must complete identity verification. This is a one-time process that typically takes under two minutes. See [Identity Verification](/getting-started/identity-verification) for the full guide.
:::

::

## Smart Wallet

When you connect to DeFlow, the platform provisions a **smart wallet** for your account. This is an ERC-4337 account abstraction wallet that enables:

- **Gasless transactions** for common platform operations
- **Batched operations** to reduce the number of on-chain interactions
- **Session keys** for streamlined deal execution without repeated signature prompts

Your smart wallet is derived from your authentication wallet and is unique to your account. You do not need to fund it separately for standard platform usage.

## Account Security

Your DeFlow account is secured through the wallet you connect during registration. There are no passwords to manage or reset. To protect your account:

- Keep your wallet recovery phrase in a secure, offline location
- Never share your recovery phrase or private keys with anyone
- Use a hardware wallet for large-value trading
- Enable any additional security features offered by your wallet provider

## Next Steps

Once your account is created, proceed to [Identity Verification](/getting-started/identity-verification) to complete the compliance check and unlock full platform access.
