Once your account is verified, you have full access to the DeFlow platform. This guide walks through the main areas of the interface and what you can accomplish in each.

## Dashboard

The dashboard is your landing page after login. It provides a real-time overview of your account activity, including:

- **Active deals** currently in progress, with status indicators for each stage
- **Lifetime volume** representing the total notional value of all deals you have settled
- **Deal count** showing how many deals you have completed
- **VIP tier** and your current progress toward the next milestone
- **Referral earnings** broken down by fee credits and cash balance

The dashboard updates in real time as deals progress through the settlement lifecycle.

## Navigation

The platform is organised around the following primary sections:

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Dashboard** | Account overview, recent activity, and key metrics |
| **Deals** | Create, manage, and track OTC trades |
| **Syndicates** | Create or join pooled investment rounds |
| **Referrals** | View your referral code, earnings, and referred users |
| **Settings** | Account preferences, verification status, wallet management |

## Creating Your First Deal

To create an OTC deal from the dashboard:

::step-guide

:::step{title="Open the Deal Wizard" n="1"}
Click **New Deal** from the dashboard or navigate to **Deals** and select **Create Deal**. The deal creation wizard opens with a guided form.
:::

:::step{title="Define the Terms" n="2"}
Specify the asset pair, notional amount, price, and settlement terms. You can set an expiry window for the deal and configure optional dispute parameters.
:::

:::step{title="Invite Your Counterparty" n="3"}
Share the deal link with your counterparty. They must have a verified DeFlow account to participate. Once they accept, the deal moves to the deployment stage.
:::

:::step{title="Deploy and Fund" n="4"}
Deploy the escrow smart contract on-chain and fund your side of the deal. The counterparty does the same. Funds are locked in the contract until settlement conditions are met.
:::

:::step{title="Settle" n="5"}
Once both parties have funded the escrow, settlement executes atomically. Both sides receive their assets in a single, irreversible transaction.
:::

::

For a detailed explanation of each stage, see the [OTC Deal Lifecycle](/user-guide/trading/otc-deal-lifecycle).

## Leaderboard

The leaderboard ranks platform users by lifetime settled volume and deal count. Rankings are pseudonymous and display only your chosen username and VIP tier badge. You can opt out of the leaderboard at any time from **Settings > Account > Leaderboard Visibility**.

## Analytics

The analytics section provides detailed metrics on your trading activity:

- **Volume over time** with monthly and cumulative views
- **Average deal size** and trend
- **Settlement velocity** measuring the average time from deal creation to settlement
- **Referral performance** including conversion rates and earnings history

## Settings

The settings panel includes the following sections:

| Tab | What you can manage |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Account** | Username, leaderboard visibility, notification preferences |
| **Verification** | Current KYC status, tier level, and re-verification triggers |
| **Wallets** | Connected wallets, smart wallet address, and session key management |
| **Security** | Active sessions and device management |
