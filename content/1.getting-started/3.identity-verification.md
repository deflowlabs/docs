# Identity Verification

DeFlow requires all users to complete identity verification before creating or participating in deals. This ensures that all counterparties on the platform meet regulatory requirements for Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance.

## Why Verification is Required

Institutional settlement requires that all participants are verified entities. Verification on DeFlow serves three purposes:

1. **Regulatory compliance** with international KYC and AML standards
2. **Counterparty assurance** so that every participant in a deal has been screened
3. **Platform integrity** to prevent fraudulent activity and protect all users

## How It Works

DeFlow partners with Zyphe, a certified identity verification provider, to conduct all KYC and AML checks. The platform itself never collects, stores, or processes personal documents.

::step-guide

:::step{title="Initiate Verification" n="1"}
After creating your account, navigate to **Settings** or follow the verification prompt on your dashboard. Click **Start Verification** to begin.
:::

:::step{title="Complete the Identity Check" n="2"}
You will be redirected to the Zyphe verification portal. The process requires:

- A valid government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID card, or driving licence)
- A live selfie for biometric matching

The check typically completes in **under two minutes**. You do not need to leave the browser.
:::

:::step{title="Automated AML Screening" n="3"}
Once your identity is confirmed, an automated AML screening runs in the background. This checks your profile against international sanctions lists, including OFAC, EU, UN, and other relevant databases. No action is required on your part.
:::

:::step{title="Verification Complete" n="4"}
When both checks pass, your account status updates to **Approved**. You can now create deals, join syndicates, and access all platform features. The verification is a one-time process and does not need to be repeated.
:::

::

## Verification Tiers

DeFlow supports multiple verification levels to accommodate different user profiles and trading volumes.

| Tier | Requirements | Capabilities |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Level 1** (Basic KYC) | Government-issued ID and biometric verification | Standard deal creation, participation in syndicates |
| **Level 2** (Enhanced) | Additional documentation, accredited investor verification | Higher deal limits, access to restricted deal types |
| **Level 3** (Corporate / KYB) | Entity verification, director checks, corporate documentation | Desk creation, partner program eligibility, institutional features |

Most individual users will only need Level 1 verification. Level 2 and Level 3 are available for users and organisations that require elevated access.

## What Data Does DeFlow Retain?

DeFlow operates under a strict [Zero-PII Policy](/security/zero-pii-policy). After verification is complete, the platform stores only:

| Retained | Not Retained |
|----------|-------------|
| Verification status (Approved, Pending, Blocked) | Name |
| KYC tier level | Photo ID images |
| AML screening result (Pass, Fail) | Address |
| AML screening timestamp (for 24-hour freshness checks) | Date of birth |
| | Email address (hashed only) |

All personal documents and biometric data remain with Zyphe and are never transmitted to or stored by DeFlow.

## AML Freshness

AML screening results expire after **24 hours**. If you attempt to fund a deal and your AML check has expired, the platform will automatically trigger a new screening before the transaction proceeds. This ensures that all participants have current compliance status at the time of settlement.

## Verification Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Unverified** | Verification has not been started |
| **Pending** | Verification is in progress |
| **Approved** | Verification is complete; full platform access is granted |
| **Blocked** | Verification failed due to sanctions screening; contact support |
| **Rejected** | Verification failed; one retry is permitted |

::callout{type="note"}
If your verification is rejected, you may retry once with corrected documentation. After three failed attempts, your account is placed into manual review. The compliance team will contact you with next steps.
::
