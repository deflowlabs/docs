## How availability labels work

- **Private beta** means the capability is available to invited participants in the current environment.
- **Limited beta** means the capability exists, but access, integration coverage, or behaviour is restricted.
- **Planned** means the capability belongs to the product model but is not an available workflow.
- **Pending review** or **pending audit** means implementation evidence must not be presented as independent assurance.

Syndicates, for example, support FIFO allocation and one escrow per approved investor in the current release. Pro-rata allocation, pooled escrow, and an on-chain SyndicateController remain planned. Availability labels and page-level notes carry this distinction without reducing the rest of the documentation to a temporary test manual.

## Change control

Every product claim is derived from the canonical product-facts ledger and has an owner and verification date. A capability changes status only when implementation, deployment, operational, and assurance evidence support the new label. Marketing plans or a visible interface alone are not sufficient.
