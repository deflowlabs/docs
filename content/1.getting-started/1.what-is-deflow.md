# What is DeFlow?

DeFlow is an institutional-grade settlement layer and asset management platform for digital asset dealflows. It enables bilateral OTC trades, multi-investor syndicate raises, and additional dealflow types with atomic on-chain execution and full regulatory compliance.

## The Problem

Settling high-value digital asset transactions today is a fragmented, trust-dependent process. Counterparties rely on manual wire transfers, ad-hoc communication, and informal agreements. There is no standard infrastructure to enforce settlement terms, verify identities, or resolve disputes programmatically.

| Challenge | How DeFlow Solves It |
|-----------|---------------------|
| OTC trades rely on counterparty trust | Smart escrow contracts enforce Delivery-versus-Payment (DvP) |
| Manual settlement via wire transfers | Atomic on-chain settlement in a single transaction |
| KYC and AML verification is siloed | Unified verification layer with status-based access control |
| No standard dispute resolution | Configurable dispute windows with governance fallbacks |
| Opaque fee structures | Transparent, tiered, on-chain fee routing |

## Core Principles

### Non-Custodial

DeFlow never holds user funds. Every deal settles through a dedicated smart escrow contract deployed on-chain. Funds are locked in the contract until both parties fulfill their obligations. The platform cannot access, redirect, or seize escrowed assets outside of the governance framework.

### Atomic Settlement

Settlement on DeFlow is atomic: both sides of a deal are executed in a single, irreversible transaction. Either both parties receive their assets, or neither does. This eliminates counterparty risk entirely.

### Zero-PII Compliance

DeFlow operates under a strict Zero Personally Identifiable Information policy. No names, email addresses, or personal data are stored in the platform database. Identity verification is handled by a certified third-party provider, and only status flags (verified, pending, blocked) are retained. Read more in the [Zero-PII Policy](/security/zero-pii-policy).

### Programmable Fees

All platform fees are routed on-chain through a transparent FeeRouter contract. Fee schedules are determined by user tier, partner rebates, and referral credits. Every fee calculation is auditable and visible before a deal is confirmed.

## What You Can Do on the Platform

### OTC Trades

Create bilateral deals with deterministic settlement. Define the asset pair, notional value, and settlement terms. Invite your counterparty, deploy the escrow contract, fund it, and settle. The entire process is governed by smart contracts.

### Syndicate Raises

Pool capital from multiple investors into a single deal. Set a target amount, define allocation terms, and share the syndicate link. Participants request allocations, the lead approves them, and settlement distributes assets proportionally.

### Analytics and Reporting

Track your personal performance, including lifetime volume, deal count, average deal size, and settlement velocity. Export data for compliance reporting or portfolio management.
