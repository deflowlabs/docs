# OTC Deal Lifecycle

Every OTC deal on DeFlow follows a deterministic five-stage lifecycle governed by smart contracts. This page explains each stage, the transitions between them, and the rules that apply at every step.

## Overview

A deal progresses through the following stages in sequence:

| Stage | What Happens | Who Acts |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Draft** | Deal terms are defined and the counterparty is invited | Creator |
| **Accepted** | The counterparty reviews and accepts the terms | Counterparty |
| **Deployed** | The escrow smart contract is deployed on-chain | Creator |
| **Funding** | Both parties deposit their assets into the escrow | Both parties |
| **Settled** | The escrow releases assets to both sides atomically | Automatic |

Once a deal reaches the **Settled** stage, it is complete and irreversible. On-chain events serve as the authoritative record of every state transition.

## Stage Details

### 1. Draft

The creator specifies the terms of the deal using the deal creation wizard:

- **Asset pair**: The two tokens being exchanged (e.g., USDC for ETH)
- **Notional amount**: The value of each side of the trade
- **Price**: The agreed exchange rate
- **Expiry window**: The maximum duration the deal can remain open before automatic cancellation
- **Dispute window** (optional): A configurable period after funding during which either party can raise a dispute

At this stage, the deal exists only in the platform database. No on-chain activity occurs.

### 2. Accepted

The creator shares the deal link with their counterparty. The counterparty reviews the terms and either accepts or declines. Acceptance requires the counterparty to have:

- A verified DeFlow account (KYC approved)
- A current AML screening result (within 24 hours)

Once accepted, both parties are committed to the deal terms. The deal is now ready for on-chain deployment.

### 3. Deployed

The creator deploys the escrow smart contract on-chain. This creates a new, dedicated escrow instance via the EscrowFactory contract using a deterministic address (CREATE2). The escrow contract is immutable once deployed: its terms cannot be altered.

::callout{type="note"}
The escrow address is computed before deployment, meaning both parties can verify the contract address independently. This ensures transparency and prevents address substitution attacks.
::

### 4. Funding

Both parties deposit their respective assets into the escrow contract. The order of funding does not matter. Each deposit is verified on-chain and recorded in the platform's event index.

Key rules during the funding stage:

- Each party can only fund their own side
- Partial funding is not permitted; the full amount must be deposited in a single transaction
- If the expiry window elapses before both parties have funded, the deal is cancelled and all deposited funds are automatically refunded
- AML freshness is re-checked before each funding transaction

### 5. Settled

Once both sides are fully funded, settlement executes automatically. The escrow contract performs an atomic swap:

- The creator receives the counterparty's assets
- The counterparty receives the creator's assets
- Platform fees are routed through the FeeRouter contract
- All events are emitted on-chain and indexed by the platform

Settlement is irreversible. The on-chain transaction serves as the permanent, auditable record of the deal.

## Cancellation and Refunds

A deal can be cancelled at any stage before settlement:

| Stage | Cancellation Rules |
|-------|-------------------|
| Draft | Creator can cancel at any time with no on-chain impact |
| Accepted | Either party can withdraw. No on-chain impact |
| Deployed | Creator can cancel. No funds have been deposited |
| Funding | If the expiry window elapses, the escrow automatically refunds all deposits. Either party can also cancel manually, triggering an immediate refund |

::callout{type="warning"}
Once a deal reaches the Settled stage, it cannot be reversed. Ensure you have reviewed the terms and verified the counterparty before funding.
::

## Dispute Resolution

For deals configured with a dispute window, either party can raise a dispute during the specified period after both sides have funded. Disputes pause the settlement process and escalate to the platform governance framework. Resolution options include:

- **Mutual agreement**: Both parties agree on an outcome, and the escrow distributes accordingly
- **Governance intervention**: The platform multisig (Safe{Wallet}) adjudicates and executes the resolution

Deals without a dispute window settle immediately upon full funding.
