# Creating a Deal

This guide walks through the complete process of creating an OTC deal on DeFlow, from opening the deal wizard to inviting your counterparty.

## Before You Begin

Ensure the following requirements are met before creating a deal:

- Your account is verified (KYC status: Approved)
- Your AML screening is current (within the last 24 hours)
- You have sufficient assets in your connected wallet to fund the deal
- You have not exceeded your concurrent deal limit (determined by your [VIP tier](/rewards/vip-tiers))

## The Deal Creation Wizard

::step-guide

:::step{title="Open the Wizard" n="1"}
From the **Dashboard** or the **Deals** page, click **New Deal**. The deal creation wizard opens as a multi-step form.
:::

:::step{title="Select the Deal Type" n="2"}
Choose the type of deal you want to create:

- **OTC Trade**: A bilateral trade between two parties
- **Syndicate Raise**: A pooled investment round with multiple participants (see Syndicate documentation for details)

Select **OTC Trade** to proceed.
:::

:::step{title="Define the Asset Pair" n="3"}
Specify the two assets being exchanged. For each side of the trade, select:

- **Token**: The ERC-20 token or native asset (ETH, USDC, WBTC, etc.)
- **Network**: The blockchain network for settlement (currently Ethereum Sepolia for Beta)

The platform validates that both tokens are supported and displays the current market reference price for comparison.
:::

:::step{title="Set the Terms" n="4"}
Configure the deal parameters:

| Parameter | Description | Default |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Your amount** | The quantity of tokens you are offering | Required |
| **Counterparty amount** | The quantity of tokens you expect to receive | Required |
| **Expiry window** | How long the deal remains open before automatic cancellation | 72 hours |
| **Dispute window** | Optional period after funding during which disputes can be raised | Disabled |

The effective price is calculated automatically from the two amounts.
:::

:::step{title="Review the Fee Summary" n="5"}
Before confirming, the wizard displays a breakdown of all applicable fees:

- **Platform fee**: The base fee percentage (typically 0.50% of the notional value)
- **VIP discount**: Any fee reduction from your current [VIP tier](/rewards/vip-tiers)
- **Referral credit**: Any credits applied from your [referral balance](/rewards/referral-program)
- **Effective fee**: The final amount you will pay at settlement

All fees are collected at settlement, not at deal creation. No upfront charges apply.
:::

:::step{title="Confirm and Create" n="6"}
Review all terms and click **Create Deal**. The deal is saved in **Draft** status. No on-chain transaction occurs at this point.
:::

::

## Inviting Your Counterparty

After creating the deal, you need to share it with your counterparty:

1. Copy the **deal link** from the deal detail page
2. Send the link to your counterparty through your preferred communication channel
3. The counterparty opens the link, reviews the terms, and clicks **Accept Deal**

::callout{type="note"}
Your counterparty must have a verified DeFlow account to accept the deal. If they do not have an account, the link will prompt them to create one and complete verification before proceeding.
::

## After Acceptance

Once the counterparty accepts, the deal transitions to the **Accepted** stage. You can then deploy the escrow contract and begin funding. See [Escrow and Funding](/trading/escrow-and-funding) for the next steps.

## Deal Management

You can manage active deals from the **Deals** page:

| Action | Availability |
|--------|-------------|
| **View details** | Any stage |
| **Cancel** | Draft, Accepted, or Deployed (before funding completes) |
| **Share link** | Draft or Accepted |
| **Deploy escrow** | Accepted |
| **Fund** | Deployed |

All deal activity is logged in the platform event index and visible in your deal history.
