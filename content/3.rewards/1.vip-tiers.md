# VIP Tiers

DeFlow rewards trading activity through a structured VIP tier system. Your tier is determined by your lifetime settled volume and unlocks progressively better fee discounts, higher deal limits, and exclusive platform features.

## How Tiers Work

- Tiers are based on **lifetime settled volume**, which is the total notional value of all deals you have settled on the platform
- Tiers **never decrease**. Once you reach a milestone, you retain that tier permanently
- Tier advancement is **automatic**. The platform recalculates your tier after every settled deal
- Your current tier and progress toward the next milestone are displayed on your dashboard and profile

## Perks by Tier

Each sub-level unlocks additional capabilities beyond fee discounts:

### Bronze (Ranks 1 to 3)

| Rank | Perk |
|------|------|
| Bronze I | Standard referral code, basic dashboard |
| Bronze II | Deal analytics unlocked |
| Bronze III | Priority support access |

### Silver (Ranks 4 to 6)

| Rank | Perk |
|------|------|
| Silver I | Two-sided referrals (earn from both sides of a referred deal) |
| Silver II | Verified badge displayed on profile and leaderboard |
| Silver III | Desk branding (custom logo and colours for your trading desk) |

### Gold (Ranks 7 to 9)

| Rank | Perk |
|------|------|
| Gold I | Pulse Analytics (advanced trading metrics and market insights) |
| Gold II | Blueprints (save and reuse deal templates) |
| Gold III | Forge API read access (programmatic data export) |

### Platinum (Ranks 10 to 12)

| Rank | Perk |
|------|------|
| Platinum I | Forge API execute access (programmatic deal management) |
| Platinum II | Dedicated support channel |
| Platinum III | **Master Rev-Share** (referral commission rate increases from 5% to 10%) |

### Diamond (Ranks 13 to 15)

| Rank | Perk |
|------|------|
| Diamond I | Dedicated account manager |
| Diamond II | Horizon Spotlight (featured placement on platform) |
| Diamond III | VIP Access Pass (invitations to exclusive events and early feature access) |

### Obsidian (Ranks 16 to 18)

| Rank | Perk |
|------|------|
| Obsidian I | Custom fee schedule (negotiated rates) |
| Obsidian II | Early access to new features and beta programmes |

### Sovereign (Rank 19)

The Sovereign rank represents the highest level of engagement on the platform. Sovereign users receive **Genesis Partner** status, which includes all perks from every preceding tier plus direct participation in platform governance discussions.

## Fee Discount Calculation

Fee discounts are applied at settlement. The discount reduces the base platform fee:

| Base Fee | Your Tier | Discount | Effective Fee |
|----------|-----------|----------|---------------|
| 0.50% | Bronze (any) | 0% | 0.50% |
| 0.50% | Silver (any) | 2.5% | 0.4875% |
| 0.50% | Gold (any) | 5% | 0.475% |
| 0.50% | Platinum (any) | 7.5% | 0.4625% |
| 0.50% | Diamond / Obsidian / Sovereign | 10% | 0.45% |

::callout{type="note"}
The effective fee can never drop below the platform revenue floor of 0.10%, regardless of stacked discounts from tiers, referral credits, or partner rebates.
::

## Leaderboard

Your VIP tier badge is displayed next to your username on the platform leaderboard. Rankings are based on lifetime volume and deal count. You can opt out of the leaderboard at any time from **Settings > Account > Leaderboard Visibility**.
