# Frequently Asked Questions

## Account and Access

### How do I create an account?

Visit the DeFlow application and click **Connect Wallet**. You can authenticate using a browser extension wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet), WalletConnect, or email/social login. During the Private Beta, an invite code is required. See [Creating Your Account](/getting-started/creating-your-account) for the full process.

### Do I need to provide personal information?

No. DeFlow operates under a [Zero-PII Policy](/security/zero-pii-policy). You choose a username, and that is the only identifying information stored. Identity verification is handled by a third-party provider, and only verification status flags are retained by DeFlow.

### What if my identity verification fails?

If your verification is rejected, you may retry once with corrected documentation. After three failed attempts, your account enters manual review. See [Identity Verification](/getting-started/identity-verification) for details on statuses and the review process.

### Can I change my username?

Yes. Navigate to **Settings > Account** to update your display name. This change takes effect immediately across the platform, including the leaderboard.

## Trading

### What assets can I trade?

DeFlow currently supports ETH, USDC, WBTC, and other ERC-20 tokens on supported networks. The list of supported assets is available in the deal creation wizard and is expanded based on demand.

### Is this real money?

Yes. Deals on DeFlow settle on live blockchain networks with real smart contracts and real assets. Start with small amounts to familiarise yourself with the process if you are new to the platform.

### What happens if a deal expires before both parties fund it?

If both sides are not funded before the expiry window elapses, the deal is cancelled automatically. Any funds already deposited are returned to the original depositor. There is no penalty for expired deals.

### Can I cancel a deal?

Yes, at any stage before settlement. During the Draft and Accepted stages, cancellation has no on-chain impact. During the Funding stage, cancellation triggers an automatic refund of any deposited funds. See [OTC Deal Lifecycle](/trading/otc-deal-lifecycle) for cancellation rules at each stage.

### What happens if a deal fails?

Escrow contracts include automatic refund mechanisms for expired or cancelled deals. If a deal cannot complete for any reason (expiry, cancellation, or dispute), deposited funds are returned to the original depositors.

### How long does settlement take?

Settlement executes automatically when the second party completes their funding transaction. The on-chain transaction typically confirms within one block. The total time from deal creation to settlement depends on how quickly both parties fund the escrow.

## Fees

### What are the platform fees?

The base platform fee is typically 0.50% of the deal's notional value. This fee is collected at settlement, not at deal creation. Your effective fee may be lower if you have a VIP tier discount, referral credits, or a partner rebate. See [VIP Tiers](/rewards/vip-tiers) for the discount schedule.

### Are there any upfront charges?

No. There are no fees for creating a deal, inviting a counterparty, or deploying the escrow contract. Fees are only charged when a deal settles.

### Can fees be reduced?

Yes, through three mechanisms:
1. **VIP tier discounts** (up to 10% off the base fee at Diamond tier and above)
2. **Referral credits** (up to 50% off the remaining fee after tier discounts)
3. **Partner rebates** (up to 25% off for qualifying partner-referred deals)

The effective fee can never drop below the 0.10% revenue floor.

## Security

### Are my funds safe?

All deals settle through on-chain escrow smart contracts. Funds are locked in the contract until both parties fulfil their obligations or the deal is cancelled. DeFlow never takes custody of your assets. See [Escrow and Funding](/trading/escrow-and-funding) for how the escrow system works.

### Can DeFlow access my funds?

No. DeFlow is non-custodial. The platform cannot access, redirect, or withdraw funds from an active escrow contract. Emergency controls (pause, seize) exist but require consensus from the governance multisig. See [Smart Contract Security](/security/smart-contract-security).

### Has the smart contract code been audited?

The smart contract suite is undergoing formal security review. Audit reports will be published on the [Smart Contract Security](/security/smart-contract-security) page upon completion.

## Rewards

### Can I lose my VIP tier?

No. VIP tiers are based on lifetime settled volume, which never decreases. Once you reach a milestone, you retain that tier permanently.

### How do referral credits work?

When your referred users settle deals, you earn a kickback split into fee credits and cash. Fee credits are applied automatically as a discount (up to 50%) on your next deal fee. See [Referral Program](/rewards/referral-program) for the complete breakdown.

### How do I withdraw my referral cash balance?

Navigate to **Referrals > Request Withdrawal**. The minimum balance is $100, and there is a 30-day cooldown between withdrawals. Cash is settled as USDC to your connected wallet via the on-chain ReferralVault contract.

### How do I opt out of the leaderboard?

Go to **Settings > Account** and toggle **Leaderboard Visibility** to off. Your username and ranking will no longer appear on the public leaderboard.
