DeFlow is a digital-asset deal coordination and settlement platform for bilateral OTC transactions and multi-investor Syndicates. It combines structured off-chain workflows with deterministic smart-contract escrow, identity and wallet readiness controls, fee and reward systems, and verifiable on-chain outcomes.

::callout{type="note"}
DeFlow is currently in private beta on Ethereum Sepolia. Availability labels identify the release state of each capability; environment-specific warnings remain visible wherever an action involves a wallet or asset.
::
